How to Use Digital Art as Gifts

The simplest route is stickers: matte or glossy sticker paper works in most home inkjet printers, and a pack of paper plus a few downloads gives you dozens of stickers for a few dollars total. If you want more durable stickers — the kind that survive a water bottle in the dishwasher — upload the design to an online sticker printer instead; the file is high-res enough for crisp results at typical sticker sizes.

For wall art, any local print shop can turn the file into a quality print, or a home printer on photo paper does the job for smaller frames. And as phone wallpapers, these designs work as-is — just download and set. A nice touch: buy the design that matches the recipient's vibe (the grumpy barista cat for your coffee-addicted coworker, the nap CEO for your perpetually-tired friend) and print a little set just for them. Personal beat expensive, every time.
