Who these gifts are for

Every family has a cat person — the one whose phone camera roll is 90% cat, whose Christmas tree is under armed guard, and who would genuinely prefer a gift about their cat over a gift for themselves. These picks cover the full spectrum: goofy tees for cat moms and dads, sticker-worthy art for the scrapbooker, and instant-download files for the last-minute shopper.

How to choose

For the jokester: look at the punchline first. "Merry Catmas Chaos" and "Stocking Head Cat: Christmas Catastrophe" are pure slapstick — great for anyone whose cat treats the tree as a personal jungle gym. "Grumpy Reindeer Kitten: Dear Santa, Define Nice" is the pick for the self-aware owner of a holiday menace.

For the aesthetic one: "Coquette Pink Christmas Kitty" leans soft and trendy, while "Ice Kitty: Frosty Whiskers Freeze-Over" and "Santa's Helper: Bow Avalanche" are more classic Christmas card vibes. Match the art to their decor and you can't miss.

For the last-minute gifter: the Ko-fi listings are instant digital downloads at $3.50 pay-what-you-want — no shipping, no waiting. Buy the file, print it at home or send it to a print shop, and it works as a framed print, a card, or a sticker sheet depending on how you print it.

Honest ordering notes

Redbubble items are print-on-demand: each piece is printed when you order it, so allow production plus shipping time if you need it under the tree by Christmas. Order tees, sweatshirts and stickers early. The Ko-fi downloads, on the other hand, are available the second you check out — they're the safety net if December sneaks up on you.
